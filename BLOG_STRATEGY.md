## 📝 Blog Post Template

```markdown
---
title: "TITLE (Deutsch)"
slug: "title-url-slug"
date: 2026-07-14
updated: 2026-07-14
author: "Virtual Marketer Team"
description: "Meta description (max 160 chars)"
keywords: "keyword1, keyword2, keyword3"
category: "AI Trends | Marketing | Use Cases"
---

# TITLE

## Introduction (2-3 paragraphs)
- Hook with relevant statistic or question
- What the post is about
- What readers will learn

## Section 1: Problem/Context
- Explain the challenge
- Why it matters now
- Industry data/statistics

## Section 2: Solution/Deep Dive
- Main insights
- How it works
- Best practices

## Section 3: Practical Application
- Real-world example or case study
- Step-by-step implementation
- Common pitfalls to avoid

## Section 4: Future Outlook
- Where this trend is headed
- Emerging applications
- Next steps for readers

## Conclusion & CTA
- Summary of key points
- Call-to-action (demo, consultation, etc.)
- Link to related posts

---

## SEO Metadata
- Internal links: 3-5 to other blog posts
- Featured image: 1200x630px, optimized
- Schema markup: Article, NewsArticle
```

## ✍️ Content Guidelines

### Tone & Voice
- Professional but accessible
- German audience (B2B focused)
- Thought leadership perspective
- Action-oriented advice

### Quality Standards
- Minimum 1,500 words per post
- Original insights (not just summaries)
- Data-backed claims with citations
- Real-world examples and case studies
- SEO-optimized for 1-2 primary keywords

### Localization
- German language preferred for main content
- English option for international audience
- Regional examples (German companies, EU regulations)
- Currency: EUR when mentioning prices

### Compliance
- GDPR-compliant data handling
- Proper attribution and citations
- No misleading claims
- Transparent about AI-generated vs. human-written content
